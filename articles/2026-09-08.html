The UK has announced sanctions on illegal Israeli settlements in the occupied West Bank, accusing Israel's government of turning a blind eye to the "ethnic cleansing" of Palestinians by settlers.

英国宣布对以色列在被占领的约旦河西岸的非法定居点实施制裁，指责以色列政府对定居者对巴勒斯坦人的“种族清洗”视而不见。

Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband said the move marked the "beginning of a new approach" to the Israeli-Palestinian conflict, which "not only calls out injustice and suffering but acts".

外交大臣埃德·米利班德表示，此举标志着对以巴冲突“新方法的开始”，这种方法“不仅指出不公正和苦难，而且采取行动”。

Israel's Foreign Minister Gideon Saar responded by accusing him of spreading an "outrageous lie" and announced counter-measures including shutting the UK's consulate in East Jerusalem.

以色列外交部长吉迪恩·萨尔回应称，他散布“无耻谎言”，并宣布反制措施，包括关闭英国在耶路撒冷的领事馆。

The UK sanctions will ban settlement goods but also act against companies and individuals providing services for settlement expansion.

英国制裁将禁止定居点商品，但也会对为定居点扩张提供服务的公司和个人采取行动。

The sanctions were launched alongside France and Canada, which in a joint statement with the UK said the action "marks a further turning point in our approach to protecting the two-state solution".

这些制裁与法国和加拿大一同推出，它们在联合声明中表示，此举“标志着我们在保护两国方案方面的方法又向前迈出了一步”。

Settlements are illegal under international law, and there has been a rise in violence towards Palestinians in the West Bank by settlers in recent months.

根据国际法，定居点是非法的，近几个月来，定居者对约旦河西岸巴勒斯坦人的暴力行为有所增加。

In a statement to MPs, Miliband said: "The British government agrees that there is ethnic cleansing of Palestinians in areas of the West Bank, perpetrated by settler terrorists.

米利班德在向议员们发表的声明中说：“英国政府同意，定居者恐怖分子在约旦河西岸部分地区对巴勒斯坦人实施了种族清洗。”

"And all too often the Israeli government has turned a blind eye to this and worse, members of it have made statements and taken actions to support the forced displacement of Palestinians."

“而以色列政府常常对此视而不见，更糟糕的是，其成员发表声明并采取行动支持强迫驱逐巴勒斯坦人。”

He said ethnic cleansing was defined by the UN as a "purposeful policy designed by one ethnic or religious group to remove by violent means another ethnic or religious group from certain areas".

他说，联合国将种族清洗定义为“一个民族或宗教群体旨在通过暴力手段将另一个民族或宗教群体从某些地区清除的有目的的政策”。

Miliband said the official view of the government was that the Israeli occupation of the West Bank was unlawful because of the "entrenchment of its control, its intention to extend permanent sovereignty, and its expansionist agenda via illegal settlements".

米利班德表示，政府的官方观点是，以色列对约旦河西岸的占领是非法的，因为其“控制根深蒂固、意图延长永久主权，并通过非法定居点推行扩张主义议程”。

He said this position, which is in line with a 2024 advisory opinion from the International Court of Justice, should be reflected in the UK's "economic relationship" with the occupied West Bank.

他说，这一立场与国际法院2024年的咨询意见一致，应反映在英国与被占领的约旦河西岸的“经济关系”中。

As a result, over the next six to nine months, the UK will introduce "a new comprehensive sanctions regime", he said.

因此，在未来六到九个月内，英国将推出“一套新的全面制裁制度”，他说。

The foreign secretary also announced a ban on advertising illegal West Bank settlements in the UK.

外交大臣还宣布禁止在英国为非法约旦河西岸定居点做广告。

"The sanctions regime will target illegal settlements and settlement expansion, not Israel," Miliband added.

“制裁制度将针对非法定居点和定居点扩张，而不是针对以色列，”米利班德补充道。

Later on Tuesday, Prime Minister Andy Burnham said the targeted measures were "aimed not at the Israeli people, but in support of the two-state solution".

周二晚些时候，首相安迪·伯纳姆表示，这些有针对性的措施“并非针对以色列人民，而是支持两国方案”。

Announcing the closure of the consulate - which represents the UK government in Jerusalem, the West Bank and Gaza - Israel's Foreign Minister Saar said at a press conference: "We know that we have many friends in Britain.

以色列外交部长萨尔在宣布关闭领事馆（该领事馆代表英国政府在耶路撒冷、约旦河西岸和加沙的利益）时，在新闻发布会上说：“我们知道我们在英国有很多朋友。”

"Unfortunately, we have a hostile Labour government that is consistently operating against the State of Israel, and it has left us no choice but to respond to its hostile actions."

“不幸的是，我们有一个敌对的工党政府，它一直反对以色列国，这让我们别无选择，只能对其敌对行动作出回应。”

He added that 11 UK MPs, including Jeremy Corbyn, Diane Abbott and all five sitting Green Party MPs, would be banned from entering Israel, accusing them of "antisemitic and anti-Israel activity".

他补充说，包括杰里米·科尔宾、黛安·阿博特和所有五位现任绿党议员在内的11名英国议员将被禁止进入以色列，指责他们从事“反犹太和反以色列活动”。

The UK also issued a joint statement with 11 other countries; France, Canada, Denmark, Spain, Finland, Ireland, Iceland, Norway, Poland, Portugal and Sweden.

英国还与法国、加拿大、丹麦、西班牙、芬兰、爱尔兰、冰岛、挪威、波兰、葡萄牙和瑞典等11个国家发表了联合声明。

It said the group was unequivocally committed to the two-state solution and would introduce national measures or support European restrictions on trading goods with West Bank settlements.

声明称，该集团明确致力于两国方案，并将采取国家措施或支持欧洲对与约旦河西岸定居点贸易的限制。

Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband announced the measures on Tuesday

外交大臣埃德·米利班德于周二宣布了这些措施

The UK announcement comes after Israel opened up tenders for the construction of some 1,200 settlement homes in the so-called E1 area - a strategically important area east of Jerusalem. The plans have drawn widespread international condemnation. Israel has held off on plans to build in the area for decades under global pressure.

英国宣布这一决定之前，以色列开放了在所谓的E1地区（耶路撒冷以东具有战略意义的地区）建造约1200套定居点住房的招标。这些计划引起了国际社会的广泛谴责。几十年来，在全球压力下，以色列一直搁置在该地区建设的计划。

Miliband told the Commons the UK's "new approach" was necessary because plans for the E1 development in the West Bank would render a two-state solution "unviable" by "cutting through the heart of Palestine".

米利班德告诉下议院，英国有必要采取“新方法”，因为约旦河西岸E1开发计划将“贯穿巴勒斯坦的心脏”，使两国方案“不可行”。

Elsewhere, he said the Hamas-led attack on southern Israel on 7 October 2023, which triggered the Gaza war, carried "terrible echoes of the centuries of persecution of the Jewish people" and stressed Israel had "the right to protect its people and defend its territory".

在其他方面，他说，哈马斯领导的2023年10月7日对以色列南部的袭击引发了加沙战争，带有“几个世纪以来对犹太人迫害的可怕回声”，并强调以色列“有权保护其人民和保卫其领土”。

However, he added, "that cannot justify what has happened in Gaza".

然而，他补充说，“这不能为加沙发生的事情辩护”。

He said: "International humanitarian law imposes clear rules for states fighting wars, including to ensure access to food and medical supplies, to avoid mass displacement of civilians, and to avoid the targeting of civilians. Serious and international violations of this law, if proven, amount to war crimes.

他说：“国际人道法为交战国规定了明确的规则，包括确保获得食品和医疗用品，避免平民大规模流离失所，以及避免以平民为目标。严重和系统地违反这些法律，如果得到证实，就构成战争罪。”

"What has occurred, including the actions of the IDF (Israel Defense Forces), have been examined in depth by a number of organisations, including the UN Independent International Commission of Inquiry.

“所发生的事情，包括以色列国防军（IDF）的行动，已经由包括联合国独立国际调查委员会在内的多个组织深入审查。”

"The conclusions of these reports represent increasing evidence that war crimes appear to have been committed, and we support legal processes to determine this."

“这些报告的结论提供了越来越多的证据，表明似乎犯下了战争罪，我们支持通过法律程序来确定这一点。”

He added it was up to international courts to determine if Israel had committed genocide in Gaza.

他补充说，以色列是否在加沙犯下种族灭绝罪，应由国际法院决定。

Israel's leaders have consistently rejected allegations of genocide, crimes against humanity and war crimes. They have insisted that Israeli forces have operated in accordance with international law.

以色列领导人一贯否认种族灭绝、反人类罪和战争罪的指控。他们坚称以色列军队的行动符合国际法。

Miliband began his statement to the Commons by referring to his Jewish roots and childhood experiences of visiting Israel, as well as recent antisemitic attacks in the UK.

米利班德在向下议院发表的声明开头提到了他的犹太血统和童年访问以色列的经历，以及英国最近发生的反犹太袭击事件。

He described himself as "a proud British Jew" and outlined his steadfast support for the state of Israel.

他称自己是“一个自豪的英国犹太人”，并概述了他对以色列国的坚定支持。

He said: "My personal story and that of thousands of British Jews is one of the many reasons why I say to anyone here or around the world who questions Israel's right to exist as a homeland for the Jewish people - you are wrong, and we will call you out whenever you engage in this attempt at the erasure of Israel."

他说：“我个人的故事和数千名英国犹太人的故事是众多原因之一，让我对这里或世界各地任何质疑以色列作为犹太人家园生存权的人说——你们错了，只要你们试图抹去以色列，我们就会指出来。”

But the UK's Chief Rabbi Sir Ephraim Mirvis said it was a "truly a dark day for British Jews", saying the sanctions were "little more than political flag bearing for mendacious anti-Israel rhetoric" that would embolden people who "use hatred as a weapon against Jews".

但英国首席拉比埃弗拉伊姆·米尔维斯爵士表示，这是“英国犹太人真正黑暗的一天”，称制裁“不过是为虚伪的反以色列言论打政治旗号”，这将助长那些“以仇恨为武器对抗犹太人”的人的气焰。

The Board of Deputies of British Jews expressed "deep regret" over Tuesday's announcement, and urged both the UK and Israeli governments to "avoid pursuing what appear to be party interests over national interests".

英国犹太人代表委员会对周二的宣布表示“深切遗憾”，并敦促英以两国政府“避免追求似乎是党派利益而非国家利益的做法”。

Responding in the Commons, Conservative shadow foreign secretary Tom Tugendhat claimed ministers were "enacting policies that the Foreign Office knows won't work".

在下议院回应时，保守党影子外交大臣汤姆·图根哈特声称，部长们“正在实施外交部知道行不通的政策”。

He agreed that illegal settlements were wrong and "some Israeli ministers have indulged in hateful propaganda and incitement" but added "this isn't about Israel alone and it certainly isn't about reducing the complexity of the region to a single sentence. Sadly, the government is in the process of doing just that".

他同意非法定居点是错误的，并且“一些以色列部长沉迷于可恶的宣传和煽动”，但他补充说，“这不仅仅是关于以色列，当然也不是把该地区的复杂性简化为一句话。可悲的是，政府正在这样做”。

Israel's foreign minister Saar said on Tuesday evening he had spoken with Tugendhat and Tory leader Kemi Badenoch, thanking them "for their firm opposition to the hostile Labour government's announcement today and its series of anti-Israel policies since taking office".

以色列外交部长萨尔周二晚间表示，他已与图根哈特和保守党领袖凯米·巴德诺克通话，感谢他们“坚决反对敌对的工党政府今天的宣布及其自上任以来的一系列反以色列政策”。

He also said British representatives from the International Gaza Support Center in Kiryat Gat in southern Israel would be removed, and UK activities to train Palestinian Authority forces in Judea and Samaria - how the Israeli government refers to the West Bank - would be terminated.

他还表示，来自以色列南部基里亚特加特的国际加沙支持中心的英国代表将被撤除，英国在朱迪亚和撒马利亚（以色列政府对约旦河西岸的称呼）训练巴勒斯坦权力机构部队的活动将被终止。

Speaking earlier on Tuesday, the country's President Isaac Herzog said the decision would "fall on the wrong side of history", adding: "In the current context, it would be a gross interference in the democratic elections of a sovereign nation."

周二早些时候，以色列总统艾萨克·赫尔佐格表示，这一决定将“站在历史的错误一边”，并补充说：“在目前的情况下，这将是对一个主权国家民主选举的粗暴干涉。”

Herzog said the move would directly harm Palestinians and urged foreign governments to turn towards "constructive dialogue" and cooperation, adding "sanctions are not a solution".

赫尔佐格表示，此举将直接伤害巴勒斯坦人，并敦促外国政府转向“建设性对话”与合作，并补充说“制裁不是解决办法”。

US Secretary of State Marco Rubio told reporters the White House had no comment on the joint action, adding that "obviously we're not going to do what the UK did".

美国国务卿马尔科·卢比奥告诉记者，白宫对联合行动没有评论，并补充说“显然我们不会做英国所做的事情”。

Earlier, Donald Trump's ambassador to Israel Mike Huckabee earlier told the BBC that the US - Israel's most powerful ally - would "without a doubt" respond to the expected sanctions, warning of a "huge economic impact on British businesses".

早些时候，唐纳德·特朗普驻以色列大使迈克·赫卡比告诉BBC，美国——以色列最强大的盟友——“毫无疑问”会对预期的制裁作出回应，并警告“对英国企业产生巨大的经济影响”。

He suggested action could be taken by individual US states as well as at a central, federal level.

他建议美国各州以及中央联邦层面都可以采取行动。

The Palestinian presidency welcomed the UK's decision, saying it was "an important step towards confronting Israel's colonial system and its violations against the Palestinian people".

巴勒斯坦总统府对英国的决定表示欢迎，称这是“朝着对抗以色列殖民体系及其对巴勒斯坦人民侵犯迈出的重要一步”。

As part of the UK announcement, Miliband also announced sanctions on the financing wing of Lebanon's Hezbollah, the Iran-backed armed group.

作为英国宣布的一部分，米利班德还宣布对黎巴嫩真主党（伊朗支持的武装组织）的金融部门实施制裁。

New sanctions in line with the US and European Union were also announced on Iran.

还宣布了与美国和欧盟一致的对伊朗的新制裁。

The intervention comes almost a year after Sir Keir Starmer's government recognised a state of Palestine, and said the UK had a moral responsibility to support a peaceful future for Israelis and Palestinians.

这一干预是在基尔·斯塔默爵士政府承认巴勒斯坦国近一年后进行的，并表示英国有道义责任支持以色列人和巴勒斯坦人的和平未来。

Israel rejects a two-state solution. It says any final settlement must be the result of negotiations with the Palestinians, and statehood should not be a precondition.

以色列拒绝两国方案。它表示，任何最终解决方案都必须是与巴勒斯坦人谈判的结果，建国不应成为先决条件。

The Palestinian Authority backs a two-state solution but Palestinian armed group Hamas does not because it is opposed to the existence of Israel.

巴勒斯坦权力机构支持两国方案，但巴勒斯坦武装组织哈马斯不支持，因为它反对以色列的存在。

Hamas says that it could accept an interim Palestinian state based on 1967 de facto borders, without officially recognising Israel, if refugees were given the right to return.

哈马斯表示，如果难民获得返回权，它可以接受基于1967年实际边界线的临时巴勒斯坦国，但不正式承认以色列。

Israel has built about 160 settlements housing 700,000 Jews since it occupied the West Bank and East Jerusalem during the 1967 Middle East war. An estimated 3.3 million Palestinians live alongside them.

自1967年中东战争期间以色列占领约旦河西岸和东耶路撒冷以来，以色列已建造了约160个定居点，安置了70万犹太人。据估计，有330万巴勒斯坦人与他们毗邻而居。

There has been a surge in attacks by settlers on Palestinians and their property in the West Bank since the start of the Gaza war, which was triggered by the Hamas-led attack on Israel on 7 October 2023.

自2023年10月7日哈马斯领导的袭击以色列引发加沙战争以来，定居者对约旦河西岸巴勒斯坦人及其财产的攻击激增。

Today's Eight Words

sanction /ˈsæŋkʃn/ n./v.

制裁；批准

An official order to restrict trade or other activities, or to approve something.

“The UK has announced sanctions on illegal Israeli settlements in the occupied West Bank.”

英国宣布对约旦河西岸被占领土上的非法以色列定居点实施制裁。

ethnic cleansing /ˈeθnɪk ˈklenzɪŋ/ n.

种族清洗

The deliberate removal of an ethnic or religious group from a certain area by force.

“There is ethnic cleansing in areas of the West Bank, says Miliband.”

米利班德表示，约旦河西岸部分地区存在种族清洗现象。

settlement /ˈsetlmənt/ n.

定居点；解决

A place where people have come to live, or an official agreement to end a dispute.

“The UK sanctions will ban settlement goods but also act against companies and individuals providing services for settlement expansion.”

英国的制裁将禁止定居点商品，同时也会针对为定居点扩张提供服务的公司和个人。

occupation /ˌɒkjuˈpeɪʃn/ n.

占领；职业

The act of taking control of a place by military force, or a job.

“Miliband said the official view of the government was that the Israeli occupation of the West Bank was unlawful.”

米利班德表示，政府的官方观点是以色列对约旦河西岸的占领是非法的。

unlawful /ʌnˈlɔːfl/ adj.

非法的

Not allowed by law; illegal.

“Miliband said the official view of the government was that the Israeli occupation of the West Bank was unlawful.”

米利班德表示，政府的官方观点是以色列对约旦河西岸的占领是非法的。

expansion /ɪkˈspænʃn/ n.

扩张；扩展

The act of becoming larger or more extensive.

“The sanctions will target illegal settlements and settlement expansion, not Israel.”

制裁将针对非法定居点和定居点扩张，而不是以色列。

displacement /dɪsˈpleɪsmənt/ n.

被迫迁移；取代

The forced movement of people from their home area.

“Members of it have made statements and taken actions to support the forced displacement of Palestinians.”

其成员发表声明并采取行动支持对巴勒斯坦人的强制迁移。

genocide /ˈdʒenəsaɪd/ n.

种族灭绝

The deliberate killing of a large group of people, especially those of a particular ethnic group or nation.

“He added it was up to international courts to determine if Israel had committed genocide in Gaza.”

他补充说，由国际法院来决定以色列是否在加沙犯下种族灭绝罪。
